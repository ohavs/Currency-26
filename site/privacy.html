Privacy Policy

Currency Converter + Widget (package com.ohav.currency) · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This app converts currencies. It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. This page explains what data is involved when you use it.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your currencies, the amount on screen, your settings (language, colours) and your conversion history are stored only on your device.
  • Exchange rates are cached on your device so the app works offline.
  • When you select text in another app and choose “Convert currency” (or share it to the app), the text is read on your device to find prices. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • If you use Android’s backup, the system may include these settings in your own Google backup. You can turn this off in your device settings.

Exchange rates

The app downloads exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (exchangerate-api.com). Like any website, that service receives your device’s IP address with the request. The request contains no personal information. See its terms and privacy policy.

Ads (version from Google Play)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • interactions with ads and the app (for example, ad views and taps, app launches),
  • diagnostics such as crash logs and performance data.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (and other places where the law requires it), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

People who buy “Premium forever” see no ads, and no ad data is collected for them.

Purchases and rewarded videos

“Premium forever” is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the app only learns whether Premium was bought, so it can unlock it. Rewarded videos are AdMob ads; the app only records that a video was watched to unlock Premium for a while.

Reviews

The app may ask once for a rating, using Google Play’s in-app review. Your rating goes to Google Play; the app does not see it.

Version from GitHub

The version published on GitHub has no ads and no purchases. It checks GitHub (github.com/ohavs/Currency-26) for new versions, which shares your IP address with GitHub.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Security and deletion

All network traffic uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because the app keeps your data only on your device, you can delete all of it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
